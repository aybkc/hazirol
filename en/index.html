2 · How much could this app have prevented?

Each event's losses are split by cause. A preparedness assistant cannot strengthen a building or stop the rain, but it can reduce losses that depend on behaviour. Only that part is calculated below. You can change the assumptions with the sliders; the result is always a range.

3 · How does it work? Prompts and validation

Step 1 · rule engine

Deterministic baseline

Bag quantities (water, food, nappies), mandatory safety rules and an AFAD-based action list are calculated in code. A plan is produced even if the AI fails.
Step 2 · generative AI

Personalisation

The model prioritises based on the household profile and adds advice specific to household members plus missing bag items. It uses schema-first JSON, a single example (few-shot) and step-by-step reasoning.
Step 3 · validation

Two-layer check

A rule-based checker catches dangerous phrases (e.g. "jump outside", "elevator", "drive through the flooded road") and made-up phone numbers; a second AI call critiques the plan against AFAD rules.

4 · Ethical assessment



  
Hallucination → wrong instructionThe model could produce deadly advice such as "go out on the balcony" during an earthquake. Safeguard: the rule-based safety list is always included; the output is scanned against a dictionary of forbidden phrases, flagged items are not shown and a warning is displayed.

  
Made-up numbers and sourcesThe model could write a wrong emergency number. Safeguard: only 112 is allowed; every other phone number in the output is removed.

  
False reassuranceSaying "your building is safe" could be deadly. Safeguard: the model may not give a verdict on building safety (negative constraint); if the building was built before 2000 or has cracks, recommending an earthquake performance assessment by a licensed firm is mandatory.

  
Vulnerable groupsSo that advice for older people, babies, people with disabilities and people on medication is not skipped, a separate item is required for each group. No medication doses or medical advice are given; users are directed to "ask your doctor".

  
PrivacyNo name, address or phone number is requested. The district field is optional. Data is not stored on a server; the plan is created only in this browser.

  
Exaggeration in the loss estimateA claim that "this many lives would have been saved" cannot be proven. Safeguard: only behaviour-related losses are counted, the result is given as a range, and all assumptions and sources are visible and adjustable; deaths caused by building collapse are not counted.




HazırOl is not an official warning system. In an emergency, call 112. For official information, follow AFAD, Turkish State Meteorological Service warnings and governorate announcements.
